Driver95 Content Studio — Privacy Policy

Updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers Driver95 Content Studio, the locally installed video creation and publishing tool operated by Dmytro Salenko for the Driver95 channel. It does not describe the separate Driver95 exam trainer. Contact: driver95.eu@gmail.com.

What the tool does

Content Studio creates educational videos from a question database and can upload videos, add them to language-specific playlists, and schedule publication using YouTube API Services. Manual export works without connecting YouTube.

Data accessed and stored

After the channel owner authorizes Google OAuth, the tool accesses the connected channel ID and name, playlist IDs and names, and the IDs, processing status, privacy status and scheduled publication times of videos it manages. It sends generated video files, titles, descriptions, tags, language, audience settings and publication times to YouTube. The requested YouTube permission allows account management; the implemented features use it for these channel and video operations.

OAuth client credentials, access and refresh tokens, channel and playlist settings, local video files, metadata and queue history are stored on the operator’s Mac. The application does not request or store the Google account password. Credentials and tokens use owner-only file permissions. They are not uploaded to this public website, GitHub, or Google Drive as part of normal application operation.

Purpose and sharing

The data is used to connect the intended channel, render the owner’s videos, upload and schedule the requested content, route videos to playlists, prevent duplicate uploads and resume the queue after interruptions. OAuth requests and API data are exchanged with Google and YouTube over HTTPS. Published videos and their public metadata are available to viewers according to YouTube visibility settings. The tool does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. No additional analytics or advertising scripts are embedded in these Content Studio information pages.

Storage and deletion

Tokens remain locally until removed or replaced, and may expire or be revoked by Google. Local exports and queue history remain on the Mac until the operator deletes them; completed queue history supports duplicate prevention. Creating a new campaign does not delete old files or YouTube videos.

To stop local processing, use Pause in Content Studio. To revoke account access, remove Driver95 Content Studio in Google Account connections. To remove locally stored credentials and authorized data, contact the operator at the address above; the operator can stop the background service and remove the local tokens, credentials and queue data. Revoking access does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube or cancel schedules already saved there. Manage or delete those videos separately in YouTube Studio.

Google policies and your choices

Google and YouTube process data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can use manual export instead of authorizing YouTube. Questions or requests about access, correction, deletion or this policy can be sent to driver95.eu@gmail.com.
