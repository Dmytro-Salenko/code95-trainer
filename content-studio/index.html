Driver95 Content Studio

A local video creation and YouTube publishing tool for the Driver95 channel.

Generate educational Code 95 videos in Ukrainian, Russian, German (Austria), Polish and English. Export videos manually or authorize the tool to upload, organize language playlists and schedule publication on YouTube.

This page describes the owner-operated desktop tool. It is not a public upload service and does not provide access to the operator’s Mac or YouTube account.

How it works

  1. Select languages, quantity and test or production mode in the locally installed application.
  2. Review the generated videos and metadata.
  3. Use manual export or connect the intended YouTube channel through Google OAuth.
  4. For automatic batches, the local queue resumes according to the configured limits and YouTube availability.

Before connecting YouTube, review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
